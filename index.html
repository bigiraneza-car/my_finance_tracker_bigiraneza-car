All about AOuii

Welcome to AOuii! Your personal pocket financial assistant. Together with AOuii you will be able to track your expenses with a simple click.


AOuii is a tool built by a student for students. It helps you record what you spend, group it by category, search for your record with patterns, and stay within the budget limit you set.
